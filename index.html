01 / About us

Pretty serious
about engineering.

BIL Barbie is FTC Team #25272, an all-girls robotics team from Almaty, established in 2023.

We design mechanisms, model components in CAD, program autonomous and TeleOp systems, manufacture prototypes and continuously improve our robot through testing.

Our work goes beyond simply assembling a robot. We collaborate, document our engineering process and introduce more students to robotics and STEM.

Engineering is our work. Personality is part of the system.
