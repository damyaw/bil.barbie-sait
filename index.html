01 — About us

Pretty serious
about engineering.

BIL Barbie was founded in 2023 by students united by robotics, technology and engineering.


We are not simply assembling a robot from existing parts. We develop mechanisms, model components in CAD, program robot behaviour, build prototypes and test our engineering solutions.


Each new version is based on what we learned from the previous one. Our goal is to combine technical precision, creativity and teamwork in one system.


We believe engineering can be intelligent, competitive — and still have personality.

01 / DESIGN

CAD & Engineering

We design robot assemblies, mechanisms and individual components before manufacturing. CAD helps us validate geometry, movement and integration before a part becomes physical.

02 / CODE

Programming

We develop robot control logic and autonomous behaviour. Our software is focused on precision, repeatability and reliable robot performance.

03 / TEST

Iteration

Build. Test. Analyse. Improve. Every prototype gives us information for the next engineering decision.
