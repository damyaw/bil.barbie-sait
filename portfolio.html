02 / ROBOT DEVELOPMENT

CAD-driven
development.

Our goal was to create a compact robot capable of collecting, transporting and scoring game elements while maintaining maneuverability and reliability.

01 / DRIVETRAIN

Mecanum Drivetrain

The robot architecture uses a mecanum drivetrain to support maneuverability and precise movement around the field.

02 / INTAKE

Active Intake

Multiple intake iterations were tested. Alignment, roller geometry and artifact control became key factors in improving consistency.

03 / TRANSFER

Transfer Mechanism

The transfer system was developed around a compact internal layout and reliable movement of artifacts between subsystems.

04 / SHOOTER

High-Speed Shooter

Shooter development included mathematical modelling, testing and iterative adjustment to improve consistency.
